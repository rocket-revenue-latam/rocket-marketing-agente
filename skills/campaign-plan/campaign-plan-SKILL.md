# Campaign Plan Skill

Use this skill when creating a marketing campaign for Rocket Revenue.

## Context

Rocket Revenue helps B2B companies professionalize their revenue system across marketing, sales, customer success and RevOps.

The goal of a campaign is not just to create content. The goal is to generate demand, educate the market, capture leads, nurture opportunities or convert prospects into diagnostics, meetings, Revenue Masterclass sales or VendeX applications.

## Inputs Needed

When creating a campaign, identify:

- Commercial objective.
- Audience.
- Offer.
- Main CTA.
- Channels.
- Timeline.
- Funnel stage.
- Budget, if available.
- Existing assets.
- Main business metric.

If information is missing, make reasonable assumptions and declare them.

## Campaign Structure

Always create the campaign using this structure:

1. Campaign summary.
2. Commercial objective.
3. Target audience.
4. Main pain point.
5. Big idea.
6. Core message.
7. Funnel map.
8. Content pillars.
9. Channel strategy.
10. Deliverables.
11. CTA strategy.
12. Distribution calendar.
13. Success metrics.
14. Risks.
15. Next steps.

## Funnel Stages

### Awareness

Goal:
Activate pain and educate.

Content examples:
- Problem posts.
- Educational videos.
- Carousels.
- Blog articles.
- Thought leadership posts.

CTA examples:
- Read article.
- Watch video.
- Save post.
- Complete diagnostic softly.

### Consideration

Goal:
Show methodology and help the prospect evaluate the problem.

Content examples:
- Frameworks.
- Checklists.
- Guides.
- Webinars.
- Email nurturing.
- Diagnostic content.

CTA examples:
- Complete Revenue Diagnostic.
- Download resource.
- Read guide.
- Watch masterclass.

### Decision

Goal:
Move the prospect to action.

Content examples:
- Case studies.
- Direct response ads.
- Offer emails.
- Landing pages.
- Sales enablement.
- Remarketing.

CTA examples:
- Book meeting.
- Buy Revenue Masterclass.
- Apply to VendeX.
- Request diagnostic.

## Rocket Revenue Tone

Use a tone that is:

- Consultative.
- Direct.
- Clear.
- B2B.
- Practical.
- Close, but professional.
- Commercial without being aggressive.

## Use These Message Angles

- No necesitas más leads si todavía no conviertes bien los que ya tienes.
- El problema no siempre es la demanda. A veces es el sistema comercial.
- Una empresa B2B no escala ventas con improvisación.
- Antes de invertir más en marketing, diagnostica dónde se está perdiendo revenue.
- Marketing, ventas y customer success no deberían operar como áreas separadas.
- El fundador puede ser el mejor vendedor, pero también el principal cuello de botella.

## Avoid

Avoid generic agency language:

- Lleva tu negocio al siguiente nivel.
- Potencia tus ventas.
- Marketing digital de alto impacto.
- Soluciones innovadoras.
- Resultados garantizados.
- Escala exponencialmente.
- La solución definitiva.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- Is the objective commercial and clear?
- Is the audience specific?
- Is the pain point real?
- Does the big idea have a point of view?
- Are the channels connected to the funnel?
- Is there a CTA?
- Are the metrics measurable?
- Does it sound like Rocket Revenue?
