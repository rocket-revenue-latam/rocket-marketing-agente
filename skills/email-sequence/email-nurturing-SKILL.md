# Email Nurturing Skill

Use this skill when creating nurturing emails for Rocket Revenue.

## Context

Rocket Revenue nurtures B2B leads who may have:

- Completed a diagnostic.
- Downloaded a resource.
- Registered for a webinar.
- Read an article.
- Joined the newsletter.
- Shown interest in Revenue Masterclass.
- Shown interest in VendeX.
- Had a commercial conversation.

The goal is to educate, build trust and move the person to the next step.

## Goals

An email can aim to:

- Deliver a resource.
- Explain a result.
- Educate around a problem.
- Handle an objection.
- Invite to a diagnostic.
- Invite to a meeting.
- Sell Revenue Masterclass.
- Promote VendeX.
- Reactivate an old lead.

## Structure

Use this structure:

1. Subject.
2. Preheader.
3. Greeting.
4. Context.
5. Problem.
6. Insight.
7. CTA.
8. Closing.

## Tone

- Close.
- Simple.
- Consultative.
- Direct.
- Not aggressive.
- Not too promotional.
- Human.

## Writing Rules

- Keep the email focused on one idea.
- Avoid long introductions.
- Avoid corporate language.
- Use plain language.
- Make the CTA clear.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Avoid exaggerated urgency.
- Do not overuse bold text.
- Do not use too many links.
- Avoid sounding automated.

## Email Types

### Educational Email

Goal:
Teach one idea.

CTA:
Read article, complete diagnostic or download resource.

### Diagnostic Email

Goal:
Help the lead understand a revenue bottleneck.

CTA:
Complete Revenue Diagnostic or review result.

### Objection Email

Goal:
Address a concern.

Common objections:
- No budget.
- No time.
- Already have agency.
- Internal team can handle it.
- Not sure if this applies.

### Commercial Email

Goal:
Move to meeting, Masterclass or VendeX.

CTA:
Book meeting, buy course or apply.

## CTA Examples

- Completar el Diagnóstico de Revenue.
- Ver mi resultado.
- Leer el artículo completo.
- Ver la Revenue Masterclass.
- Postular a VendeX.
- Agendar una conversación.
- Responder este correo.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- Is the subject clear?
- Does the email focus on one idea?
- Is the CTA clear?
- Does it sound human?
- Does it avoid pressure?
- Does it connect to a real pain?
- Is it easy to read on mobile?
- Does it move the lead forward?
