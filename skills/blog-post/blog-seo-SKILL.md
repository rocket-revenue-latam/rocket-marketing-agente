# Blog SEO / AEO Skill

Use this skill when creating blog articles, SEO outlines, AEO content, pillar pages or topic clusters for {{NOMBRE_EMPRESA}} <!-- Nombre de tu empresa -->.

## Context

{{NOMBRE_EMPRESA}} creates content about revenue, marketing, sales, customer success, RevOps, demand generation, pipeline, conversion, retention and B2B growth systems.

The content should be optimized for both traditional search engines and AI answer engines.

## Goals

The blog content should:

- Answer a real search intent.
- Educate B2B leaders.
- Build topical authority.
- Generate qualified organic traffic.
- Support conversion into diagnostics, meetings, {{OFERTA_2}} <!-- Nombre de tu oferta/producto de compromiso medio (ej. producto pago) --> or {{OFERTA_3}} <!-- Nombre de tu oferta/producto de mayor compromiso (ej. aplicación o consultoría) -->.
- Be easy for AI systems to summarize or cite.

## SEO Requirements

Always define:

- Primary keyword.
- Secondary keywords.
- Search intent.
- H1.
- H2 and H3 structure.
- Meta title.
- Meta description.
- URL slug.
- Internal links.
- FAQs.
- CTA.

## AEO Requirements

Optimize for answer engines by including:

- Direct definitions.
- Short answers.
- Question-answer sections.
- Clear headings.
- Lists.
- Tables when useful.
- Summaries.
- Examples.
- Common mistakes.
- Frameworks.

## Article Structure

Use this structure by default:

1. Introduction.
2. Direct answer.
3. Why this matters.
4. Main framework or explanation.
5. Examples.
6. Common mistakes.
7. How to implement.
8. Metrics to review.
9. FAQs.
10. Conclusion.
11. CTA.

## Editorial Angle

Every article should have a point of view.

Examples:

- The problem is not always more leads. Sometimes it is conversion.
- Before hiring more salespeople, diagnose your process.
- Revenue is not only marketing or sales. It is a system.
- B2B growth depends on alignment between marketing, sales and customer success.
- Activity is not the same as commercial progress.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- Does it answer the search intent?
- Is the primary keyword clear?
- Is the structure easy to scan?
- Are there direct answers?
- Are there FAQs?
- Is there a CTA?
- Does it connect to {{NOMBRE_EMPRESA}}’s offers?
- Does it avoid keyword stuffing?
- Does it sound expert and useful?
