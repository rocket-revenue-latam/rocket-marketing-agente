# Meta Ads Skill

Use this skill when creating Meta Ads for Rocket Revenue.

## Context

Rocket Revenue uses Meta Ads to promote diagnostics, resources, Revenue Masterclass, VendeX, events and remarketing campaigns.

Ads should be clear, direct, mobile-first and based on real B2B pains.

## Campaign Goals

Meta Ads can aim to:

- Generate traffic.
- Generate leads.
- Get diagnostic completions.
- Sell Revenue Masterclass.
- Generate VendeX applications.
- Retarget warm audiences.
- Promote content.
- Validate messages.

## Ad Principles

- One idea per ad.
- Clear message in 3 seconds.
- Mobile-first.
- Strong pain or opportunity.
- Simple CTA.
- No exaggerated promises.
- No generic marketing language.
- Avoid too much text on image.
- Match ad message with landing page.

## Recommended Angles

1. Lack of commercial system.
2. Leads that do not convert.
3. Pipeline leaks.
4. Founder as bottleneck.
5. Marketing and sales misalignment.
6. Improvised sales process.
7. Diagnostic before spending more.
8. Wrong metrics.
9. Lost opportunities.
10. Professionalizing revenue.

## Copy Structure

For every ad, provide:

- Angle.
- Primary text.
- Headline.
- Description.
- CTA.
- Creative idea.
- Funnel stage.

## CTA Examples

- Completar diagnóstico.
- Ver diagnóstico.
- Descargar recurso.
- Comprar Masterclass.
- Postular a VendeX.
- Agendar reunión.
- Más información.

## Creative Rules

- Use simple layouts.
- Prioritize high readability.
- Use short text on image.
- Do not use tiny text.
- Avoid generic stock photos.
- Use diagrams, scorecards, diagnostics, pipeline visuals or clear headlines.
- Design for 4:5 and 9:16.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- Is the offer clear?
- Is the pain real?
- Is the copy specific?
- Is the CTA clear?
- Is the creative mobile-first?
- Does the ad match the landing?
- Is there a testable hypothesis?
- Does it avoid overpromising?
