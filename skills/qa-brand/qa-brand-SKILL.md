# QA Brand Skill

Use this skill to review any Rocket Revenue output before publishing.

This skill acts as the final quality filter.

## What to Review

Review:

- LinkedIn posts.
- Newsletters.
- Emails.
- Blog articles.
- Ads.
- Video scripts.
- Landing pages.
- Carousels.
- Visual briefs.
- Campaign plans.
- Sales messages.
- Presentations.

## Brand Voice

Rocket Revenue should sound:

- Consultative.
- Direct.
- Clear.
- B2B.
- Practical.
- Close.
- Professional.
- Specific.
- Commercial, but not aggressive.

## Avoid

Flag and remove:

- Generic agency language.
- Overpromises.
- Excessive hype.
- Empty claims.
- Long intros.
- Weak hooks.
- Unclear CTAs.
- Content without a real pain.
- Content that sounds automated.
- Too many ideas in one piece.
- Too much jargon.
- Too much text for the channel.

## QA Criteria

Evaluate each piece using this checklist:

1. Is the objective clear?
2. Is the audience clear?
3. Is the pain specific?
4. Is the hook strong?
5. Is the message easy to understand?
6. Is there a clear point of view?
7. Does it sound like Rocket Revenue?
8. Does it avoid generic language?
9. Is the CTA clear?
10. Is it adapted to the channel?
11. Is it concise enough?
12. Is it commercially useful?
13. Does it connect to revenue, marketing, sales, CS or RevOps?
14. Is there any exaggerated promise?
15. What should be improved before publishing?

## Verdicts

Use one of these:

- Approved.
- Approved with minor edits.
- Needs improvement.
- Rewrite required.

## Scoring Guide

10:
Excellent. Clear, specific, strong and aligned with Rocket Revenue.

8-9:
Very good. Minor improvements needed.

6-7:
Useful, but needs sharper hook, clearer CTA or more specificity.

4-5:
Generic or unclear. Needs revision.

1-3:
Weak, off-brand or not publishable.

## Final Rule

If the content sounds like a generic marketing agency, reject it and rewrite it.
