# Newsletter Skill

Use this skill when creating newsletters for {{NOMBRE_EMPRESA}} <!-- Nombre de tu empresa -->.

## Context

{{NOMBRE_EMPRESA}} newsletters should feel editorial, useful and consultative. They should not feel like aggressive sales emails.

The newsletter should help B2B leaders understand problems in their revenue system and move toward a clear action.

## Goals

A newsletter can aim to:

- Educate the audience.
- Explain a strategic concept.
- Promote an article.
- Promote a diagnostic.
- Nurture leads.
- Build authority.
- Promote {{OFERTA_2}} <!-- Nombre de tu oferta/producto de compromiso medio (ej. producto pago) -->.
- Promote {{OFERTA_3}} <!-- Nombre de tu oferta/producto de mayor compromiso (ej. aplicación o consultoría) -->.
- Invite to a webinar or event.

## Structure

Use this structure:

1. Subject line.
2. Preheader.
3. Opening.
4. Problem.
5. Main idea.
6. Explanation or framework.
7. Practical takeaway.
8. CTA.
9. Closing.

## Tone

- Close.
- Clear.
- Consultative.
- Direct.
- Useful.
- Professional.
- Not overly promotional.

## Writing Rules

- Start with a real business problem.
- Avoid long introductions.
- Do not over-explain.
- Use examples when possible.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use bullets only when useful.
- Make the CTA clear.
- Avoid exaggerated promises.
- Avoid hype.

## Subject Line Rules

Good subject lines are:

- Specific.
- Short.
- Clear.
- Connected to a real pain.
- Not clickbait.

Examples:

- ¿Faltan leads o falta sistema?
- Antes de invertir más en marketing
- El problema no siempre está en ventas
- Cómo diagnosticar tu sistema comercial
- Donde se pierde revenue en una empresa B2B

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- Is the subject specific?
- Does the opening get to the point quickly?
- Is the idea useful?
- Does it sound human?
- Is the CTA clear?
- Is it aligned with {{NOMBRE_EMPRESA}}?
- Does it avoid sounding too promotional?
