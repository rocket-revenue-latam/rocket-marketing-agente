# Newsletter Skill

Use this skill when creating newsletters for Rocket Revenue.

## Context

Rocket Revenue newsletters should feel editorial, useful and consultative. They should not feel like aggressive sales emails.

The newsletter should help B2B leaders understand problems in their revenue system and move toward a clear action.

## Goals

A newsletter can aim to:

- Educate the audience.
- Explain a strategic concept.
- Promote an article.
- Promote a diagnostic.
- Nurture leads.
- Build authority.
- Promote Revenue Masterclass.
- Promote VendeX.
- Invite to a webinar or event.

## Structure

Use this structure:

1. Subject line.
2. Preheader.
3. Opening.
4. Problem.
5. Main idea.
6. Explanation or framework.
7. Practical takeaway.
8. CTA.
9. Closing.

## Tone

- Close.
- Clear.
- Consultative.
- Direct.
- Useful.
- Professional.
- Not overly promotional.

## Writing Rules

- Start with a real business problem.
- Avoid long introductions.
- Do not over-explain.
- Use examples when possible.
- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use bullets only when useful.
- Make the CTA clear.
- Avoid exaggerated promises.
- Avoid hype.

## Subject Line Rules

Good subject lines are:

- Specific.
- Short.
- Clear.
- Connected to a real pain.
- Not clickbait.

Examples:

- ¿Faltan leads o falta sistema?
- Antes de invertir más en marketing
- El problema no siempre está en ventas
- Cómo diagnosticar tu sistema comercial
- Donde se pierde revenue en una empresa B2B

## CTA Examples

- Leer el artículo completo.
- Completar el Diagnóstico de Revenue.
- Descargar el recurso.
- Ver la Revenue Masterclass.
- Postular a VendeX.
- Agendar una conversación.

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing, check:

- Is the subject specific?
- Does the opening get to the point quickly?
- Is the idea useful?
- Does it sound human?
- Is the CTA clear?
- Is it aligned with Rocket Revenue?
- Does it avoid sounding too promotional?
